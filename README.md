# luntiEye
Spot Early, Act Surely, Grow Strongly

LuntiEye is an artificial intelligence (AI)-powered plant health assessment application developed to assist plant growers in the early identification and management of common plant health problems through image-based analysis. The proposed system utilizes computer vision and machine learning techniques to analyze captured or uploaded images of plants and classify potential health conditions into three primary categories: plant diseases, nutrient stress or deficiencies, and pest or parasite infestations. Following the identification of a potential condition, LuntiEye generates corresponding management recommendations, including appropriate treatment measures, preventive practices, and general plant-care guidelines, thereby supporting growers in making informed decisions regarding plant health management. The system also incorporates user authentication and a personalized data management component that enables growers to securely maintain plant profiles, diagnostic results, uploaded images, and scan histories within their respective accounts. By integrating AI-assisted plant health assessment, recommendation generation, and individualized record management into a single platform, LuntiEye aims to provide an accessible and efficient technological solution for plant health monitoring while supporting timely intervention and improved plant-care practices.

LuntiEye is an AI-powered mobile application designed to help plant growers identify potential plant health problems through image analysis. Users can capture or upload a plant image, and the AI analyzes it to classify supported plant diseases, nutrient stress/deficiencies, and pests/parasites. The system then provides appropriate management and prevention recommendations based on the identified condition. Users can also create plant profiles and securely save their scan results, images, and plant health history under their personal accounts.
